# Photography sources

All website photographs are real photographs of UFV Chandigarh and SD College students in India, published by the University of the Fraser Valley. They are illustrative images, not images of BreakevenFX participants. No claim is made about the pictured students’ incomes. No generated images are used.

All photographs: University of the Fraser Valley, CC BY 2.0. Files were downloaded through Wikimedia Commons. Images are displayed with CSS cropping; pixels, faces, and subjects have not been altered.
