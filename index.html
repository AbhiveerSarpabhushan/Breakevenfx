// THE MISSION

Financial literacy is the most underpriced asset in rural India.

Millions of brilliant young minds grow up hours away from the nearest brokerage, with no one to explain a candlestick, a SIP, or what compounding can do across a decade.

BreakevenFX closes that gap. We bring institutional-grade market education to villages — live workshops, real trading labs, and one-on-one mentorship — so the next generation of Indian investors isn't decided by pincode.

⇗
Real capital, real stakes

Students learn to raise, manage, and grow actual money — $400K and counting.
