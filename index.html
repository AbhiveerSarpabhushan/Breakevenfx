BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

A few good questions.

Who is the mentorship for?+

Our focus is low-income college students in India who want to understand investing and build their financial knowledge. No investing background is needed.

What will I learn?+

We focus on financial foundations, understanding investing and risk, and developing your own judgment through personal guidance.

Is this a stock-tips service?+

BreakevenFX is an investing education and mentorship community. We help students understand financial ideas and make informed decisions. We don’t offer trading signals or promises of investment returns.
